    Our story

    Dinner should sound like something

    We opened Bites & Beats because the best meals we'd ever eaten were the loud ones — a bar in San Sebastián, plates arriving faster than anyone could order them, nobody sitting down.

    So we built a room with an open fire, a long marble bar and a sound system that earns its keep. The food is serious. The evening isn't.
